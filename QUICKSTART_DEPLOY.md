# 🚀 Quick Deployment Reference\n\n**TL;DR** - Deploy your gaming website in under 5 minutes using Render.\n\n---\n\n## 🎯 Fastest Way to Deploy\n\n### Step 1: Go to Render\n```\nhttps://render.com\n```\n\n### Step 2: Sign In with GitHub\n- Click \"Sign up\"\n- Choose \"GitHub\"\n- Authorize Render\n\n### Step 3: Create Web Service\n- Click \"New +\" → \"Web Service\"\n- Select your `YNGSAVICE/gaming-website` repository\n- Choose `main` branch\n\n### Step 4: Configure\n```\nName: gaming-website\nEnvironment: Docker\nPlan: Free (pause after 15 min inactivity)\nRegion: Choose closest to your location\n```\n\n### Step 5: Add Database\n- Click \"Create Database\" (in same region)\n- Choose PostgreSQL\n- Plan: Free\n- It will auto-set `DATABASE_URL`\n\n### Step 6: Set Environment Variables\n```\nFLASK_ENV=production\nOPENWEATHER_API_KEY=<get-from-openweathermap.org>\nSECRET_KEY=<auto-generated>\nJWT_SECRET_KEY=<auto-generated>\n```\n\n### Step 7: Deploy!\n- Click \"Deploy\"\n- Wait 5-10 minutes\n- Your app is live! 🎉\n\n**URL:** `https://gaming-website-xxxxx.onrender.com`\n\n---\n\n## 📋 What You'll Need\n\n### Before Deploying\n- [ ] GitHub account with your repo\n- [ ] OpenWeatherMap API key (free at https://openweathermap.org/api)\n- [ ] Render account (free)\n\n### Environment Variables Needed\n```\nFLASK_ENV=production\nSECRET_KEY=any-random-string\nJWT_SECRET_KEY=any-random-string\nOPENWEATHER_API_KEY=your-openweathermap-key\nDATABASE_URL=auto-set-by-render\n```\n\n---\n\n## 🌐 Add Custom Domain (Optional)\n\n1.
